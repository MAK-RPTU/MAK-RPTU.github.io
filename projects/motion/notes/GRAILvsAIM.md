GRAIL produces the better-looking robot motion — your perception is correct. Measured on the same two clips through both pipelines: GRAIL's trajectories have ~2× lower jerk (0.1 vs 0.2 and 0.5 vs 0.9 krad/s³) and half the peak joint velocity on calm content (3.7 vs 7.6 rad/s). That's because its pipeline drops the feet to the floor (hard ground contact) and its retarget stage applies RL-grade filtering. Smoothness and grounding dominate what you see in the sim.

AIM has the more accurate human-motion recovery — by published benchmark. On EMDB-2 world-coordinate metrics, PromptHMR (AIM's recon, CVPR 2025) beats GVHMR (GRAIL's recon): WA-MPJPE 71.0 vs 111.0 mm (36% lower error) and root-trajectory error 1.3% vs 2.0%. AIM is also ~2× faster per clip and handles multi-person and moving-camera footage better (SAM2 tracking + DROID-SLAM).
